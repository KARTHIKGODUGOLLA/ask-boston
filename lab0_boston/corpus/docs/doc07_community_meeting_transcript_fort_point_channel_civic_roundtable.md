## DOCUMENT 7: COMMUNITY MEETING TRANSCRIPT — FORT POINT CHANNEL CIVIC ROUNDTABLE

**Date:** June 10, 2026 · **Location:** Meridian Wharf Café, 486 Congress Street
**Facilitator:** Priya Raman · City interpreter present (Spanish / Haitian Creole / Chinese)

**PRIYA RAMAN:** Thank you all for coming. Tonight we have Deirdre Fitzgerald of Harborline Structural Group to walk us through the bridge study, and then the floor is yours.

**DEIRDRE FITZGERALD:** The short version: the swing span is in critical condition, and our recommendation is to bring it back for people walking and biking — not vehicles. The design money is moving through the council now. We know the fencing and the debris have been neighborhood problems, and interim fixes are funded.

**YOLANDA PEÑA:** Cuando el puente estaba abierto, la gente cruzaba caminando desde el centro y compraba pan de camino al trabajo. Desde que lo cerraron en diciembre de 2014, los sábados vendo la mitad de lo que vendía antes. *(Interpreter: Ms. Peña says the bakery lost its downtown foot traffic when the bridge closed.)*

**WILNER JOSEPH:** Mwen se kapitèn Seaport Skiff. Depi mwa janvye, mwen oblije deplase pontonn mwen an de fwa akoz debri k ap tonbe soti nan pon an. Si yo pa ranje baryè yo, yon moun ka blese. *(Interpreter: Capt. Joseph raised safety concerns about debris near his landing float.)*

**GRACE YUEN:** My couriers walk everything the long way now, around by Seaport Boulevard. 如果條橋重開，行路送餐可以快二十分鐘。For hot food, twenty minutes is the whole business.

**DEIRDRE FITZGERALD:** On the debris — netting is going over the fender system this summer, and both approach gates get rebuilt under the interim line. That should reach the float and the courier route both.

**CAMILA PEÑA:** I spoke at City Hall last month about benches and shade on the new deck. Is that actually in the design scope, or just noted?

**DEIRDRE FITZGERALD:** In scope. Deck furnishings are part of the 100 percent documents.

**PRIYA RAMAN:** We'll post the study summary here at the café. Same table, second Wednesday, next month.
