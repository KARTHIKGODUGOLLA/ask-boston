## DOCUMENT 3: BOSTON CITY COUNCIL — COMMITTEE MEETING MINUTES (EXCERPT)

**Committee on Streets, Bridges & Waterways**
**Date:** May 19, 2026 · **Location:** City Hall, Committee Room 5
**Present:** Chair Odessa Klein; Councilors Ruthanne Bailey, Linh Duong, Rafael Otero, Declan Fitzgerald

**Docket #0457 —** Order authorizing an FY2027 capital appropriation for design and engineering, Northern Avenue Bridge Rehabilitation.

**Presentation.** Deirdre Fitzgerald, PE, principal of Harborline Structural Group, summarized the firm's May 2026 condition assessment: the swing-span superstructure carries a condition rating of 2 — "critical" — on the federal 0–9 scale; rehabilitation in a pedestrian- and cyclist-only configuration is estimated at $95 million in 2024 dollars; construction would run approximately 30 months from notice to proceed.

**Disclosure and recusal.** Councilor D. Fitzgerald disclosed that the presenting engineer is his sister and recused himself from deliberation and from the vote on Docket #0457.

**Questions.** Councilor Bailey asked whether interim fencing was funded; Ms. Fitzgerald referred the committee to the FY2027 interim fencing and security line ($310,000). Councilor Duong asked about debris entering the channel; Ms. Fitzgerald described planned netting over the fender system.

**Public comment.** C. Peña, Fort Point resident, urged the committee to include shade structures and benches in the new deck design. Written testimony from W. Joseph, owner of Seaport Skiff Water Taxi, regarding debris near his landing float, was entered into the record.

**Action.** The committee voted 4–0, with one recusal, to recommend Docket #0457 favorably to the full council.
