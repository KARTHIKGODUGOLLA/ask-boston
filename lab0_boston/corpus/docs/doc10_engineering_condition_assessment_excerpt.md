## DOCUMENT 10: ENGINEERING CONDITION ASSESSMENT (EXCERPT)

**Northern Avenue Bridge — Condition Assessment and Reopening Feasibility Study**
**Prepared for:** City of Boston, Public Works Department
**Prepared by:** Harborline Structural Group · Principal-in-charge: Deirdre Fitzgerald, PE (MA #48291)
**Submitted:** May 11, 2026

**1. Structure.** The Northern Avenue Bridge is a 1908 rim-bearing swing bridge over the Fort Point Channel: three riveted Pratt through-trusses on a central turntable pier, with a timber fender system protecting the pier and approaches.

**2. Service history.** The structure was closed to vehicular traffic in 1997 and closed to all users in December 2014. It has remained fenced at both approaches since.

**3. Findings.**
- Swing-span superstructure condition rating: **2 (critical)** on the NBI 0–9 scale.
- Section loss of up to 60 percent in floor-beam bottom flanges over the channel spans.
- Fatigue cracking at riveted lower-chord connections at six locations.
- Scour to a depth of 1.4 meters at the turntable pier; fender timbers are actively shedding debris into the channel.
- Perimeter fencing breached at both approaches; field crews observed evidence of recurring unauthorized access on the swing span.

**4. Recommendation.** Rehabilitate the span as a pedestrian- and cyclist-only crossing. Rehabilitation cost is estimated at $95 million in 2024 dollars; construction duration is approximately 30 months from notice to proceed. Interim measures: rebuild both approach gates and install debris netting over the fender system ahead of construction.
