## DOCUMENT 2: BIOGRAPHICAL DOSSIERS — REOPENING STUDY FIGURES

### PROFILE: Deirdre Fitzgerald, PE
**Position:** Principal Structural Engineer, Harborline Structural Group
**Office:** 12 Farnsworth Street, Fort Point
**Licensure:** Professional Engineer, Massachusetts (License #48291)
**Education:** BS Civil Engineering; MS Structural Engineering

**Professional History:** Fitzgerald joined the City of Boston Bridge Operations Division in 2009 as a field engineer, inspecting movable and fixed spans across the harbor district. She left the city in 2019 to found Harborline Structural Group, a nine-engineer practice specializing in historic steel structures. She is principal-in-charge and lead author of the 2026 Northern Avenue Bridge condition assessment.

**Family:** Brother, Declan Fitzgerald (Boston City Councilor, District 2).
**Languages:** English (native).

### PROFILE: Declan Fitzgerald
**Position:** Boston City Councilor, District 2
**Elected:** November 2025

**Professional History:** A former high-school civics teacher, Fitzgerald chairs no committees in his first term but sits on the Committee on Streets, Bridges & Waterways. His district office fields a steady stream of constituent cases about the fenced-off Northern Avenue Bridge.

**Family:** Sister, Deirdre Fitzgerald (structural engineer).
**Languages:** English (native).

### PROFILE: Yolanda Peña
**Position:** Owner, Gull & Anchor Bakery, 47 Sleeper Street, Fort Point
**Born:** Veracruz, Mexico

**Professional History:** Peña moved to Boston in 2004 and worked as a pastry cook in two downtown hotel kitchens before opening Gull & Anchor Bakery in March 2011. The bakery is known for its anise-seed harbor rolls and for feeding half the studio buildings on the block.

**Family:** Widowed. One daughter, Camila Peña (17), a high-school junior and school-year co-op intern at Harborline Structural Group.
**Languages:** Spanish (native), English (fluent).

### PROFILE: Wilner Joseph
**Position:** Owner and Captain, Seaport Skiff Water Taxi
**Born:** Port-au-Prince, Haiti

**Professional History:** Joseph arrived in Boston in 2010. He launched Seaport Skiff Water Taxi in May 2021; the service runs small electric launches on the harbor edge and berths at a seasonal float on the Fort Point Channel behind 486 Congress Street.

**Languages:** Haitian Creole (native), French (fluent), English (fluent).

### PROFILE: Grace Yuen
**Position:** Owner, Golden Terrace Catering, Chinatown

**Professional History:** Yuen founded Golden Terrace Catering in 2016. The kitchen delivers lunch service across the Fort Point Channel to Seaport offices; with the Northern Avenue Bridge fenced off, her walking couriers detour along Seaport Boulevard.

**Languages:** Cantonese (native), Mandarin (fluent), English (fluent).

### PROFILE: Priya Raman
**Position:** Managing Member, Meridian Wharf Holdings LLC; Operator, Meridian Wharf Café
**Address of record:** 486 Congress Street, Fort Point

**Professional History:** Meridian Wharf Holdings LLC owns the building at 486 Congress Street. Raman opened the ground-floor Meridian Wharf Café in 2018 and convenes the Fort Point Channel Civic Roundtable there each month.

**Languages:** English (native), Tamil (conversational).
