## DOCUMENT 6: BUDGET NARRATIVE EXCERPT — FY2027 STREETS CABINET

**FY2027 Recommended Budget, Volume II — Capital Plan Narrative (excerpt)**

The FY2027 plan commits $3.65 million to design and engineering for the Northern Avenue Bridge rehabilitation, advancing the pedestrian- and cyclist-only concept recommended by the 2026 condition assessment. Design will carry the historic swing span's rehabilitation through 100 percent construction documents, including deck, railing, and lighting elements shaped by community input gathered in Fort Point.

A companion line funds interim fencing and security at the closed structure at $310,000, addressing recurring reports of breached gates on both approaches. Elsewhere along the channel, the plan carries $1.25 million for the full reconstruction of Sleeper Street and $860,000 for Harborwalk decking repairs, keeping the waterfront path continuous while bridge work advances.
